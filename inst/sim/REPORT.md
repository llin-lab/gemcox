## 3. Conventions

- **Primary metric:** the paired per-replicate difference in contrast
  recovery, GeM-Cox (gamma = 1) minus each comparator, with MCSE from the
  paired SD.
- **Normalised gain:** (arm - gamma0) / (oracle - gamma0), a ratio of
  paired means with a delta-method MCSE.
- **Random-direction floor:** sqrt(2 / (pi p)), reported as description
  only.
- **Degenerate fit:** a fitted cluster with fewer than 10 subjects or fewer
  than 5 events.
  - The primary analysis excludes degenerate fits.
  - A sensitivity analysis scores them at the floor.
  - Rates are given for every method in every cell
    (`results/tab_degeneracy.csv`).
- **Fixed settings:** gamma = 1, lambda = 0.05, alpha = 0,
  `normalize_gmm_by_dim = FALSE`, event rate 0.55, k-means initialisation.

#### E5d: type I error when profiles separate but mechanisms do not (added after sim-freeze-v1)

- *Setting:* profile-only null (K_true = 2, beta_sep = 0), n = 400,
  p = 10; default LRT with the bootstrap null, B = 19.
- *Expectation recorded in advance:* rejection within 2 MCSE of 0.05 in
  every cell. A cell fails if its rate exceeds
  0.05 + 2 sqrt(0.05 x 0.95 / datasets).

| null | n | rho | mu_sep | datasets | rejection at 0.05 | threshold | KS p | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E5c reference (mu_sep = 0) | 400 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 500 | 0.052 (0.010) | 0.0695 | 0.043 | (reference) |

| profile-only | 400 | 0.0 | 0.5 | 500 | 0.070 (0.011) | 0.0695 | 0.033 | FAILS (exceeds 0.05 + 2 MCSE) |
| profile-only | 400 | 0.0 | 1.0 | 500 | 0.052 (0.010) | 0.0695 | 0.055 | calibrated |
| profile-only | 400 | 0.0 | 2.0 | 500 | 0.046 (0.009) | 0.0695 | 0.069 | calibrated |
| profile-only | 400 | 0.5 | 1.0 | 200 | 0.060 (0.017) | 0.0808 | 0.024 | calibrated |

**Verdict: the expectation fails.**
- At mu_sep = 0.5 the rejection rate was 0.070 (35 of 500), above the
  0.0695 threshold. The other three cells were within tolerance.
- By the rule recorded in advance, the test is **not validated for data
  with profile structure**; no fix was attempted.
- The excess is small. For a calibrated test, P(>= 35 of 500) is 0.030 per
  cell and about 0.09 for at least one of the three 500-dataset cells. So
  chance is not ruled out, and neither is a real inflation.
- E5e's first sequential step, on different datasets, points the same
  way: 0.090 (0.020) at mu_sep = 1, n = 400, against E5d's 0.052 for the
  same design.

## 5. Findings that disagree with expectations or earlier claims

1. **An engine defect behind earlier results.** The `get_cumhaz_at()`
   indexing defect also existed inline in `log_surv_density()` in
   `GeMCox_combined.R`, the E-step survival term.
   - It fired in 158 of 200 simulated datasets and corrupted nearly every
     subject's term.
   - It affected every gamma > 0 result produced with that file, including
     the evidence cited for the "settled" decisions.
   - The package uses the corrected engine; its tests would catch the
     defect.
2. **Which test to use.** The earlier permutation test claimed "type I
   0.049 by construction; power 17-25%".
   - With a nonzero shared effect, the cross-validated statistic with the
     permutation null rejected at 0.112 under the null, and had power
     0.05-0.10.
   - The default is now the likelihood ratio with the bootstrap null:
     0.052 under the null, power 0.30-0.57 at beta_sep = 2 (n = 400-800).
   - At about 52 events (n = 117) it had only 0.13-0.14 power.
3. **P2 depends on replication** (Section 2).
4. **Mechanism correlation (E4b)** was 0.334 at gamma = 1 against 0.089 at
   gamma = 0. The earlier figure was 0.68.
5. **E4c softness.** The earlier result was Spearman -0.90 across
   configurations. Here:
   - the fully sharpened configuration collapsed to one cluster in 200 of
     200 fits, so it is reported separately;
   - the remaining configurations are reported individually.
6. **Decay of the gain with dimension** was roughly p^-1.15 (E3b), against
   the earlier p^-1.05.
7. **normalize = TRUE and ARI.** Under profile separation it lowered ARI
   from 0.548 to 0.119 (p = 10) and from 0.335 to 0.027 (p = 80). The
   earlier figures were 0.700 to 0.002 (different design).
8. **S1's recorded expectation** was "normalize = TRUE better only at
   mu_sep = 0 with large p". TRUE was better at mu_sep = 0 for **both**
   p = 10 and p = 80; FALSE was better at mu_sep = 2, as expected.
9. **The random-direction floor is not the no-information level.**
   - Two-stage fell below it and gamma = 0 rose slightly above it at large
     beta_sep.
   - Two-stage's below-floor recovery came entirely from degenerate fits:
     a tiny cluster whose Cox fit was zeroed, making the "contrast" equal
     the other cluster's coefficients. Such fits align with the shared
     coefficient (|cos| 0.93-0.97) with or without heterogeneity (post hoc
     diagnostic).
   - Non-degenerate two-stage fits sit near the floor.
10. **Two-stage degenerates often.** About 40% of fits at mu_sep = 0 and
    n = 400, 88% at n = 200, and 100% at p >= 40. So there are no valid
    two-stage pairs at p >= 40 under the primary rule.
11. **Prediction.** When profiles were identical, GeM-Cox's error in
    predicting the true linear predictor was no smaller than a single Cox
    model's (E2, mu_sep = 0). Better contrast recovery did not translate
    into better prediction, because new subjects' membership comes from
    features alone.
12. **Adaptive normalisation (E2b).**
    - It raised contrast recovery by 0.085 (0.004) on average.
    - It was worse in one cell (mu_sep = 1, beta_sep = 1: -0.030, 0.011),
      so under the pre-registered rule FALSE stays the default.
    - It roughly doubled the test's power at n = 400-800.
    - It made the test anti-conservative at n = 117 (0.135 under the
      null).
13. **K selection (E5b).** The joint cross-validated criterion selected
    K = 2 in 100% of datasets with profile separation and in 0-0.5% of
    datasets with mechanism-only differences. This is consistent with the
    earlier finding that K is selectable only when profiles separate.
14. **Reproducibility defects, fixed during the study.**
    - mclust initialises from a random subset when n > 2000, and the
      stream was not seeded per method. The E4a two-stage values are from
      the seeded rerun.
    - All pipeline random draws use the L'Ecuyer-CMRG generator. A
      replicate refitted in a session using R's default generator did not
      reproduce until each per-dataset function pinned the generator.
    - `gemcox_simulate(seed = s)` in a default session gives different
      data from the pipeline's.

15. **E5d fails its expectation (added after sim-freeze-v1).**
    - With profile-only structure the default test rejected at 0.070
      (0.011) at mu_sep = 0.5, above the pre-registered threshold 0.0695.
    - It is therefore not validated for data with profile structure.
    - Earlier sections validated it only at mu_sep = 0.
16. **K = 3 can be selected, but only with strong separation (E5e).**
    - With strong separation in both profiles and mechanisms, K = 3 was
      selected in 93-100% of datasets by the joint criterion, BIC and
      the sequential LRT, and in 79-85% by the partial criterion.
    - With strong profile separation alone, the joint criterion selected
      it in 100% of datasets, and BIC in 100% at n = 800 but 21% at
      n = 400. The partial criterion and the sequential LRT selected
      K = 1, which is correct for their question.
    - At moderate separation or with mechanism-only differences no
      selector recovered it reliably.
    - This refines the earlier "K <= 2 only" statement, which rested on
      tests that could not select K > 2.
17. **E5b's "K is selectable when profiles separate" holds only for
    strong separation (E5e).** E5b tested only mu_sep = 3. At mu_sep = 1
    the joint criterion selected K = 1 in 97-99.5% of profile-only
    datasets.
18. **The selectors answer different questions (E5e).**
    - The joint criterion and BIC count feature-profile clusters.
    - The sequential bootstrap LRT is the only one sensitive to mechanism
      differences without profile separation.
    - BIC's penalty made it select K = 1 in every "both, moderate"
      dataset.
    - The partial criterion selected K >= 2 in 13% of null datasets.
19. **The adaptive-normalisation test (E5f)** was anti-conservative up to
    about 88 events (0.080 at n = 200) and at the nominal level by about
    132 events (0.050 at n = 300).

## 8. Limitations

- **Scope of K:**
  - Estimation accuracy is validated for K <= 2.
  - Selection of K = 3 was tested (E5e) and works only with strong
    separation.
  - Contrast recovery at K = 3 was not evaluated.
- **Features:** Gaussian, and independent except in the rho = 0.5 cells.
- **Validity of the default test:** calibrated when profiles do not
  separate (mu_sep = 0). Not validated when they do (E5d).
- **Scale:** one sample size per experiment except E3a and E4a. The
  CVIA078-scale results come from three cells only.
- **Coarse p-values:** null distributions used B = 19 replicates, so each
  p-value is on a 1/20 grid. Rejection rates are exact for that B but
  individual p-values are coarse.
- **Proportional hazards:** every model and data-generating mechanism
  assumes proportional hazards within clusters.
